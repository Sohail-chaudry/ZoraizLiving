Who We Are

Our Story

A family brand born from love for fragrance, craft, and the timeless art of beautiful living — proudly made in Pakistan.

A Name Rooted
in Family

Zoraiz is the name of our nephew — a name we carry with pride into every bottle, every bar, every sculpture we create. When we started this brand, we knew we wanted it to carry a name that meant something deeply personal to us. Zoraiz was that name.

Zoraiz Living is not just a store. It is a family's love for craft, culture, and the art of beautiful living. We are based in Pakistan and we are proud of it. Every product we make celebrates the richness of our heritage — from the Mughal perfumery tradition to the botanical wisdom of our grandmothers.

What We Make

Three Worlds. One Soul.

🧴
Zoraiz Ittars

Our flagship line of alcohol-free attars and perfume oils, blended using traditional methods with premium ingredients. Every bottle is a piece of olfactory poetry — halal, pure, and eternal.

🧼
ZL Botanicals

Handcrafted organic soaps made from natural botanical extracts, pure oils, and time-tested recipes. Each bar is an act of self-care — shaped by hand, scented by nature.

🏺
ZL Craft

Artisan home décor pieces featuring animal motifs — each one sculpted by hand, finished with care, and designed to bring a quiet nobility to any living space.
